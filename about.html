About Feastly

We help you get dinner on the table, calmly.

Feastly is an independent site about the most familiar meal of all: dinner, cooked at home for the people around your table.

We focus on the parts that cause the most stress: how much to cook, when to start and how to put together a menu that feels balanced. Our tools give fast answers and our guides explain the thinking behind them.

How we write

Quantities and timings come from tested home cooking and established food-safety guidance. Every guide is checked by a second editor and dated. We do not sell food, equipment or ingredients, and nobody pays to be mentioned.
